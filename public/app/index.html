01 / About

Not a finished star, but a voice being discovered.

ChaeA writes from the space between English and Korean, memory and melody. Her music stays close to the room: notebook pages, a small microphone, acoustic guitar, and the kind of Seoul light that makes a quiet lyric feel almost visible.
